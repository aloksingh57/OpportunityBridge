# OpportunityBridge · 4-minute demo runbook

Use fictional local demo content only. No live employers, hiring activity, email delivery, AI provider, or external database is configured.

1. **Discover (45 sec):** Open `/`; point out the demo-data banner, the student/founder/mentor/investor community, and the primary marketplace action.
2. **Explore (45 sec):** Open `/opportunities`, search “React”, combine filters, inspect an opportunity, and save it as a demo user.
3. **Apply (40 sec):** Sign in as Aarav Sharma (Student), apply to a listing, then open Applications and show the saved `Submitted` status.
4. **Publish (50 sec):** Switch to Sara Khan (Recruiter) or Kabir Malhotra (Founder), publish a demo listing, and review the publisher applicant workspace.
5. **Connect (45 sec):** Open People, send/accept a request, start a conversation with a connected profile, and show the persisted messages/notifications.
6. **Explain (35 sec):** Open Recommendations and describe the visible scoring weights and reasons. Switch to another role to show the tailored dashboard.
7. **Trust (20 sec):** Show that `/admin` requires a separate server-only key and that fictional sample data is labeled as demo content.

The exact home-page greeting and seeded application statuses are derived from the current sample dataset. Do not describe demo records as live vacancies, actual employment outcomes, or verified organizations.
