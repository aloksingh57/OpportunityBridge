# OpportunityBridge · 4-minute demo runbook

Use fictional local demo content only. No live employers, hiring activity, email delivery, AI provider, or external database is configured.

1. **Discover (45 sec):** Open `/`; point out the demo-data banner, the student/founder/mentor/investor community, and the primary marketplace action.
2. **Explore (45 sec):** Open `/opportunities`, search “React”, combine filters, inspect an opportunity, and save it as a demo user.
3. **Apply (40 sec):** Sign in as Aarav Sharma (Student), apply to a listing, then open Applications and show the saved `Submitted` status.
4. **Investor discovery (30 sec):** Open `/startups`, filter fictional startups by industry or stage, and follow a founder profile.
5. **Publish (40 sec):** Switch to Sara Khan (Recruiter) or Kabir Malhotra (Founder), publish a demo listing, and review the publisher applicant workspace.
6. **Guide and connect (55 sec):** Request a CV or pitch review from a mentor/advisor, accept it from the provider profile, and (optionally) upload a small sample PDF. Explain that the provider cannot access the file before accepting.
7. **Network and trust (35 sec):** Open People, send/accept a request, start a conversation, and show report/block controls. Switch to Settings to manage blocked profiles.
8. **Explain (25 sec):** Open Recommendations, point out visible scoring reasons, then show `/admin`'s server-key gate and private report review queue.

The exact home-page greeting and seeded application statuses are derived from the current sample dataset. Do not describe demo records as live vacancies, actual employment outcomes, or verified organizations.
